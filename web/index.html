Business view

What does fraud cost?

Each missed fraud loses its real transaction amount plus a chargeback fee. Each alert costs an analyst review. For every model, the threshold with the lowest total cost is found automatically.

Costs are computed on the test set ( transactions). In production, pick thresholds on a separate validation set.
